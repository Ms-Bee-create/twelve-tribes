What everything actually does, in plain language.

Inside the Camp
The Tent
The heart of the camp. Every upgrade you finish anywhere raises Family Power, and expanding The Tent itself raises your hero's max stamina. Needs a matching Muster level to upgrade further.
Muster
Trains troops — Shieldbearers (front line), Archers (mid line), Riders (back line). It's rock-paper-scissors: Shieldbearers beat Archers, Archers beat Riders, Riders beat Shieldbearers. Higher tiers (II, III) unlock as Muster and Camp level up and hit much harder. A balanced formation beats a big pile of one type. Each training batch starts at 500 troops at Muster level 1 and grows to around 100,000 by level 100 — Drill Instructors research grows it further and trains faster.
Healer's Tent
Treats wounded troops. Losing a fight mostly wounds troops (they end up here until treated) but some are killed outright and don't come back — attacking losses run about 60% wounded/40% killed, defending losses about 80% wounded/20% killed.
Storehouse
Raises how much lumber, grain, water, and oil you can hold at once. Anything gathered past the cap just stops counting, so it's worth keeping ahead of your income.
Oil Press
Presses oil automatically all the time — no hero needed. Oil is what fuels Break Camp (moving your whole camp to a new spot on the map).
Scribes' Tent
Unlocks Research — permanent camp-wide bonuses (faster gathering, more storage, faster healing and training) that take real time to complete. Leveling it up unlocks new projects and higher levels of existing ones.
Forge
Buy Sandals, a permanent hero speed upgrade. Skins of Wine (restore stamina) are sold at the Market now.
The Market
Buy Speedup Tokens (use them on any active timer, up to an Instant-Finish Token that completes one outright), Resource Packs, temporary Deployment Capacity Boosts, and convenience items (Skins of Wine, Hero XP Boosts, Gear Material Bundles).
Chieftains
Level up your heroes one at a time. A higher-level hero hits harder in every fight, no matter what they're sent against.
Provisions
Your item inventory and a running tally of resources gathered. Use items — like a Skin of Wine — straight from here.
The Gathering
Your recurring rewards live here: the Daily Check-In, the current Weekly Push milestones, and one-off Contracts.
Lumber Yard, Threshing Floor & Cistern
Passive resource buildings — up to 3 plots each, producing lumber, grain, or water automatically by the hour once built. No hero needed, just build and upgrade plots over time. The site itself also has its own level, separate from its plots — upgrading it boosts every plot's output by 15% per level. Different from the resource tiles out on the map, which still need troops sent out to farm them.
The Watchtower
Every report the camp generates — gathering, combat, building — sorted into tabs so you can actually find what you're looking for.
Out on the Map
Sending Troops
How many troops one march can carry is capped, not unlimited — it scales with Camp level (1,000 at level 1, up to about 200,000 at level 100), plus a small bonus for total research completed. The formation screen shows exactly how many of that cap you're using as you fill it in.
Resource tiles
Send a hero with a formation to farm one. More troops sent means faster gathering. A tile drains as it's farmed, then goes quiet for a while and relocates somewhere new nearby.
Raiders, rival camps & Raider Camps
Scout first (free), then raid. Losing costs stamina and nothing else; winning pays out silver and sometimes resources. Named rivals and generic Raider Camps both relocate after being defeated.
Syndicates
Attackable solo — no troops required, just a hero. Killing one has a chance to drop Forge crafting materials, and each syndicate icon always drops materials for the SAME gear item: claws → Blade of the Tribe, blade → Ward of the Covenant, fist and skull → Banner of Endurance (two sources for that one). Generic scattered targets share the same 4 icons, so they're just as farmable as the 4 named ones.
The City Gate
Whoever holds it collects a steady bonus until someone else takes it. Contested by everyone — signed in or not.
